COIN gate Q4 — why the M2 diff (doctrine march speeds) reads RED, and which kind of red (2026-07-03)
====================================================================================================
Question (coordinator): A) harness pacing debt, B) genuine design consequence (8 days too
short at honest speeds), or C) M2 defect (tax bites where it shouldn't)?

VERDICT: A — HARNESS PACING DEBT, with the Q1 relief lottery riding on top. B and C refuted
with numbers below.

C REFUTED (tax only bites real grades) — from M2's own doctrine-pace evidence
(doctrine-pace-m2.txt, 8 staged COP->village marches):
  flat-ish routes (ascent 2-17 m): sim/FM ratio 1.03-1.23  <- AT or ABOVE the FM rate; a
    tax spuriously biting terrace micro-grades would push these well BELOW 1.0
  the real climb (Donga, 249 m ascent): ratio 0.95         <- exactly doctrine-priced
  mean 1.09 overall (was 1.42 pre-tax). The grade probe reads the 5 m elevation field
  along the walk direction (combat.ts GRADE_PROBE) and the tax formula is the cited
  Naismith<->FM 3-97.6 load interpolation. No defect found.

B REFUTED (movement does NOT eat the day) — per-day time budget of the careful policy's
op squads under M2, survey-1, 8-day tour (coin-q4-budget-m2-s1.txt):
  TOTAL sq1: assemble 0.2h  march 5.5h  onstation 7.0h  return 8.0h  IDLE 165.3h
             -> march+return 7% of tour time, onstation 4%, IDLE 89%
  TOTAL sq2: march+return 11%, onstation 6%, IDLE 83%
  A KLE at honest speeds costs ~4.3 h door-to-door (was ~3.5 h at trunk speeds); the
  daylight alone could hold 2-3 such ops. The squads are idle ~20 h/day.

A CONFIRMED (the once-a-day scheduler is the ceiling):
  The harness scheduler sampled ONCE PER GAME-DAY at the midnight tick; any op straddling
  it idled its squad the rest of the day. Tempo was pinned at <=1 op/squad/day at ANY
  movement speed — so the careful policy's COIN delivery ran at a fraction of its own
  design intent (FM 3-24 sustained daytime population engagement), leaving margins so
  thin that M2's honest speeds + one bad trajectory roll dropped the surviving seed's
  score under the gate bar.
  Throughput under M2 (old pacing), survey-1: patrols 7 (1 sched-miss), KLEs 8, projects
  1/3 — essentially UNCHANGED from trunk (8/8, 2/3). What actually moved the score
  36 -> 11 was the Q1 lottery: a single day-7 catastrophe (+12 KIA in one day; tour KIA
  21 -> 27, coin-q4-diag-m2-s1.txt) — combat-trajectory chaos, not movement throughput.

THE FIX (scripts/campaign-loop.ts — harness only, zero new tuned constants):
  Event-driven scheduling: the schedulers run every strategic step; an op squad steps off
  again when it is home+free — DAYLIGHT-gated for careful (shuras convene in daylight; a
  careful commander does not push presence patrols into the night; gate = the sim's own
  isNight()), ungated for body-count (night ambushes are its character). TOC-level acts
  (CERP funding, secure dispatch, resupply) also become event-driven (all already
  idempotent-guarded). Tempo now EMERGES from op duration + the daylight window + the
  muster/rest machinery. Justified from design intent; no ops/day count exists to tune.

THE HONEST PRICE OF DOCTRINE SPEEDS (report, don't narrow — harness law): under equal
