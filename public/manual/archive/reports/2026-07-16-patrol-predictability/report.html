The predictability tax measures zero — and the instrument that proved it wrong is the win

The enemy learned to remember where you walk, and the same-day verdict said the scripted careful commander was being punished for walking fixed routes. We built the probe the issue asked for. It refuted the issue: six IEDs planted, zero detonated, across six full tours — the men are dying to something else entirely.

01The claim, and the number it got

The enemy-network wave gave the insurgency a decaying patrol-heat memory (a 32×32 grid that integrates wherever US/ANA boots move) and taught the IED emplacer to bury its charge on the hottest cell ahead of a patrol. Same day, the COIN gate's careful mean collapsed 85.7 → 33.3, and issue 037 was filed with a story: the adaptive enemy punishes the harness's maximally-predictable fixed-route commander.

The probe runs the gate's full careful commander twice per seed — same KLEs, same CERP, same secured builds, same tight ROE — with exactly one decision changed: 1st Squad's approach axis, fixed every visit vs rotated ±240 m (three heat buckets) per visit.

Refuted. Route hygiene changes nothing on the current enemy, because the things killing the careful tours — ambush and harassing fire — spawn around the patrol's live position. You cannot out-route an enemy that materializes relative to where you are. The one channel that genuinely uses memory (heat-sited IEDs) never connects.

02Why the signature opener never fires

The emplacer guesses where you are going by drawing a ray from the COP through the patrol and sampling 30–95 m ahead (director.ts:117-175). Against that guess: an 8 m victim trigger (combat.ts:1574), a triggerman whose patience runs out after roughly 104 s (ai/insurgent.ts:64), and a 600 s dud timeout. A real A* path curves with the terrain, so the guess almost never passes within eight metres of the boots — the cell compromises or melts away, and the charge is culled with it. Six plants, six duds.

The geometry of a dud: a straight-line guess against a curved world, graded by an eight-metre trigger and a hundred-second patience.

Filed as issue 038 with design directions (command-detonation at closest approach; placement on the historical hot corridor instead of a forward guess) — a sim change deserving its own gate cycle, deliberately not made in this pass. The probe is already the instrument that will judge it: when the channel works, plants > 0, detonations > 0, and fixed-vs-varied hit-rate should finally split.

05So what actually collapsed the mean? Difficulty.

On two of three gate seeds the network-era enemy delivers ~5 KIA per day against a tight-ROE, daylight-only, route-varying careful commander — a ~36-man platoon combat-ineffective inside a week. A tour that survives to end-of-tour still scores 0 on casualty penalties (survey-0 varied: endDay 9, score 0). For scale: the entire Korengal campaign, 2006–2010, cost roughly fifty American lives; the sim's hot seeds spend that in nine days from one platoon — two orders of magnitude hotter than the fiction the design is built on.

Whether that is the intended difficulty is the owner's call, now queued in issue 030's calibration scope with the levers named: ambush frequency and lethality under the network's cell proximity-staging and aggression personalities — not the heat/IED system. Nothing was tuned in this pass; the harness law stands (casualties are a diagnostic, never a target).

06Instrument lessons, paid for in CPU

Three findings from the probe's own construction, recorded so nobody pays twice:

COIN is the coolant. A "purified" patrol-only policy (no KLE, no CERP) let the valley heat into near-continuous contact and the adaptive stepper correctly ran fine-grained almost permanently — ~50 seconds of wall per game-hour. The strategy layer isn't just the win condition; it is what keeps a careful tour affordable to simulate.

Rotation cycles must start offset. A {0,+1,−1} per-village cycle never engages on a 1–3-visit tour: the first A/B smoke came back byte-identical (same 35 KIA, same 210 contacts) because every k was zero. The shipped cycle is {+1,−1,0}.

Heat is dwell, not transit. The raw heat field is dominated by elements holding ground — a secure element parked on a project site banked 249,455 bucket-seconds — so the concentration stat can't isolate one squad's route hygiene even with the COP excluded. Honest residual.

07Residuals, named

Variety covers sq1's presence lane only (KLE and secure routes remain direct). The casualty A/B carries full trajectory-reshuffle noise (n=3 seeds; enough to refute "route hygiene explains the collapse," not enough to bound a small tax if 038 later makes the channel lethal — re-run the probe then). The blast-attribution proxy went unexercised (nothing detonated). And the hot-seed gate legs are expensive because the valley is in constant contact — the difficulty question and the gate-cost question (issue 030) are the same fact wearing two hats.
