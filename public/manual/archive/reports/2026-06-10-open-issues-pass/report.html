Closing the Open Issues — three shipped, one measured revert, two characterized

A single pass over every open issue in the backlog, run the way this repo demands: metricize before touching code, prove every win on held-out seeds, adversarially verify before calling it done — and, just as importantly, revert on the numbers when a change doesn't earn its place. The headline isn't only what shipped; it's that the same rigor that landed three realism wins also stopped a fourth from regressing the game, and turned two "perf" tickets into honest decisions instead of busywork.

Reverted — on the numbers

007 · Aspect-driven vegetationreverted

proven: forest-faces-north 59% → 73% · scrub-faces-south 48% → 70% — but: clean A/B KIA 1.17 → 1.83 (+56%) AND stranded a unit

Forest should cling to shaded slopes and scrub own the sunny ones, and an aspect term in classifyLand made it so — measurably. But it touches the cover/conceal field the insurgents' steep-face ambush positions and the concealment-biased pathing read: a clean same-code balance A/B regressed KIA +56% and stranded a unit. For a subtle visual gain, an unshippable regression. Reverted; the probe and the exact gate that keeps the COP clean are kept for a future balance-revalidated pass.
